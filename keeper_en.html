📌 Common Issues

Q. KeepericonがAutofill is not working
→ Click the Keeper icon and use the "→ Fill" button next to the record matching the current URL.
Q. Chromeの「passwordsSave?」keeps appearing
→ Check that Step 3 (disable Chrome password manager) has been completed.
Q. A device approval screen appeared after login
→ Refer to the Device Approval section in Step 1. If you have no other device, message Slack #help_社内システム or #global-corporate-it.
Q. Import but fewer passwords imported than expected
→ Importduring「Select All」→「Keeper to Keeper」was completed。
